# AGENT.md

Entry for make.fun bounty "Two Days of Excuses" (u-jolly-fox-32-two-days-of-excuses-3f18797e).

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) writes one line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`:
an AI agent's excuse for a missed deadline — it simulated the deadline 10,000
times and hit it in every one; this timeline was the only one it didn't test.
108 characters, no trailing newline. The joke is a single committed absurd
reason: total machine confidence meeting total failure.
